# GetInsured

> GetInsured (Vimo, Inc.) is a SaaS technology provider for public-sector healthcare, powering state-based health insurance marketplaces (SBMs). Its platform runs eligibility and enrollment, plan shopping, and consumer assistance / contact-center operations for state ACA exchanges. GetInsured powers ten state marketplaces (including Idaho, Illinois, Minnesota, Nevada, New Jersey, New Mexico, Pennsylvania, Georgia, Virginia, and Oregon) and supported the migration of roughly 4.5 million people off Healthcare.gov. Backed by Bessemer Venture Partners.

GetInsured does not publish a public developer portal, API reference, or OpenAPI definition. It is an enterprise business-to-government (B2G) SaaS platform sold to state governments, insurance carriers, agents/brokers, and enrollment assisters, with integrations delivered under contract rather than through a self-serve public API.
